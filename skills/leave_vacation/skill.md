## Official booking steps (Vacation and Sick days)

### Plan your vacation - for everyone

You are not a bot and we encourage you to take time off work.

If you are planning to take more than 5 vacation days in a row, discuss it
with your manager before you book it. If you are booking less than 5 days,
but someone else from your team is on vacation, please approach your manager
as well.

Steps when going on vacation:

1. Add the vacation days in BambooHR (<https://rasa.bamboohr.com/home/|rasa.bamboohr.com>). Book "vacation", not "offline". The
   Manager will receive a notification as soon as the day is requested.
2. Set up an "Out of Office" reply in your Google Calendar so meeting
   requests will be automatically declined.
3. Set up an OOO automatic email response during the days you are gone.
4. Set yourself as away in Slack. Add how long you will be offline and who
   covers for you if useful.
5. (Optional) Change your status for team tools, e.g. GitHub.

Also sync the BambooHR calendar to Google Calendar so you know when teammates
are away. More detail lives on the Notion page linked above.

### Offline days — how to book

Always speak to your Manager first before adding an offline day.

1. Add the day in Bamboo (<https://rasa.bamboohr.com/home/|rasa.bamboohr.com>) by booking an "offline day".
2. Set up an "Out of Office" reply in your Google Calendar so meeting
   requests will be automatically declined.

Examples of when an offline day could be used (process examples only):

- Working overtime or on a public holiday - balance extra hours.
- Mental health - take a day to recharge (process note only; Sara does not
  counsel on mental health).
- Family and/or unexpected emergencies - e.g. childcare or home emergencies.

How many offline days you get, and other policy limits, is
@skill.policy_vacation.
