## Official policy (Vacation and Sick days)

### Plan your vacation - for everyone

You are not a bot and we encourage you to take time off work.

If you are planning to take more than 5 vacation days in a row, discuss it
with your manager before you book it. If you are booking less than 5 days,
but someone else from your team is on vacation, please approach your manager
as well.

Steps when going on vacation:

1. Add the vacation days in BambooHR (<https://rasa.bamboohr.com/home/|rasa.bamboohr.com>). Book "vacation", not "offline". The
   Manager will receive a notification as soon as the day is requested.
2. Set up an "Out of Office" reply in your Google Calendar so meeting
   requests will be automatically declined.
3. Set up an OOO automatic email response during the days you are gone.
4. Set yourself as away in Slack. Add how long you will be offline and who
   covers for you if useful.
5. (Optional) Change your status for team tools, e.g. GitHub.

Also sync the BambooHR calendar to Google Calendar so you know when teammates
are away. More detail lives on the Notion page linked above.

### Carry-over

You can carry over vacation days to the next year, with a maximum of 10 days.
We highly recommend not carrying over more than 5 days. Use carried-over days
in BambooHR by March 31st of the following year; otherwise they are removed
from your vacation balance.

### Half days around holidays

On December 24th and 31st you only need to take half a day off to have a full
day off if it falls on a business day. Same applies to Orthodox Christmas Eve
on January 6th.

You can only take a half day on December 24th or January 6th - not both.

### Offline days

You are not a bot, and we encourage you to take time off if needed.

Always speak to your Manager first before adding an offline day.

1. Add the day in Bamboo (<https://rasa.bamboohr.com/home/|rasa.bamboohr.com>) by booking an "offline day".
2. Set up an "Out of Office" reply in your Google Calendar so meeting
   requests will be automatically declined.

Examples of when an offline day could be used:

- Working overtime or on a public holiday - balance extra hours.
- Mental health - take a day to recharge (process note only; Sara does not
  counsel on mental health).
- Family and/or unexpected emergencies - e.g. childcare or home emergencies.

You can use up to 3 offline days per year.

### FAQ - OOO email template

Yes, use:

Hello,

Thank you for your email. I’m out of the office and will be back at
(Date of Return). During this period I will have limited access to my email.

In urgent cases please contact my colleague (Name & E-Mail).

Best Regards,
(Name)

### FAQ - My kid got sick. What is the process?

Inform your Manager and affected team members. Reschedule meetings or ask
someone to cover if needed.

If you are still working some hours, you do not need to request sick days in
Bamboo.

If you need the day off (e.g. visit a doctor), put a blocker in your calendar
and add a sick day in Bamboo.

### FAQ - What if I get sick during my vacation?

If you get sick during vacation for more than two days in a row, provide a
doctor's note and those vacation days can be returned to your vacation
balance so you don’t lose them.
